# CLAUDE.md

## What is this?

This project is a **website for a product MVP** that doubles as a **first sales and
investor pitch**. The audience is prospective **co-founders and investors**.

The goal is **not** to show a perfect or finished product, but to **present** the
venture convincingly and attract the right people. The website is a showcase, not
the final product.

## Purpose of the website

Two things must stick with the viewer:

1. **Vision & market opportunity** — why this venture is big and relevant.
2. **Clear product & demo** — what the product concretely does and how it feels.

Whenever a design or copy decision comes up, the guiding question is always:
*"Does this move a co-founder or investor closer to a yes?"*

## Scope (MVP)

Three pages, no more:

1. **Product** — what it is, what problem it solves, why now.
2. **Function** — how it works, core features, demo/screens if available.
3. **Vision + Team** — where this is headed (market, opportunity) and who is behind it.

## Tech stack

- **Plain HTML / CSS / JS** (static, no framework, no build step).
- Clean structure, readable, easy to extend.
- Responsive (desktop + mobile) — the pitch has to land on a phone too.
- No unnecessary dependencies. External libraries only when they add real value.

## Tone & design

- Professional, clear, trustworthy — investors are reading.
- Focused and minimal: one strong statement beats ten weak ones.
- Substance over buzzwords. Show concretely what the product can do.
