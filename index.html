For restaurants, cafés & bars

Flip it. Your team
already knows.

Tiltable is a tabletop call device for restaurants, cafés and bars. No app to open, no QR code to scan — guests flip it 180° and your staff sees a green light from anywhere in the room.

The core idea

Three steps. Zero training.

1. Guest flips the device

A quiet 180° turn on the table. No app to open, no button to hunt for.

2. LED turns green

Full brightness, visible from across the room — in daylight or dim evening lighting.

3. Service arrives

The nearest available server sees the call and clears it when it's handled.

The signal

A light that's impossible to miss

Flip the device over and a green LED lights up immediately, at full brightness — visible in daylight and in dim evening service alike. It's the entire interface: no screen to read, no icon to interpret. Just on, or off.

  • Idle: off, or a dimmed red.
  • Called: solid green until a server clears it.

Line of sight

Visible from anywhere in the room

The LED runs around the full edge of the device, not just the top face. Whether your staff is at the pass, behind the bar, or clearing a table across the room, the call reads clearly from any angle — not only when looking straight down at the table.

Zero friction

Nothing for guests to install

No app to download, no QR code to scan, no Wi-Fi password to ask for. Guests already know how to flip something over — it's about the most intuitive interface there is. It works correctly on the first try, for every guest at the table.

Power

Charges itself, ready every shift

Devices sit in a charging tray behind the bar or at the host stand. Drop them in at closing, pull them out at opening — there's no cabling to individual tables and nothing for staff to manage during service.

Built for service

Built for spills, drops and every wipe-down

A sealed shell with no buttons or ports on the tabletop surface — wipe it down between covers like anything else on the table. It's built to take the same knocks and spills as the rest of your service ware.

Setup

Connects to the Wi-Fi you already have

Every device joins your existing Wi-Fi network — no separate router, hub or proprietary base station to install. Add a table, remove a table, rearrange the floor for a private event: the fleet follows without a technician visit.

Why it matters

What it means for your floor

Faster to the table

Guests are noticed the moment they flip the device — not after minutes of waving or catching an eye.

More table turns

Faster response means faster orders and faster payment — tables free up sooner during peak hours.

Less walking, more serving

Staff stop doing constant round-the-room checks "just in case" and respond to actual calls instead.

Calmer service, better tips

Attentive without hovering. Guests feel looked after; staff feel less frantic during the rush.
