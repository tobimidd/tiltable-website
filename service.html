Server wearable — concept

It only buzzes
for your tables.

Each server wears a small device that vibrates only for calls from their own section — table number front and center, nothing else to check.

Section logic

Only your tables reach your wrist

Sections are assigned per shift from the dashboard. When table 7 flips its device, only the server responsible for table 7 feels it — everyone else's wearable stays silent. No more group scrambles when one table calls.

Form factor

Still an open question

Three directions we're weighing. All concept stage — none of this is finalized.

Concept stage

Wristband

Worn like a fitness band, on the wrist all shift.

Always on the wrist — hard to misplace

Hands stay completely free

Smaller display for table numbers

Shared hardware needs a charging routine

Concept stage

Smartwatch

A dedicated watch-style display, purpose-built.

Larger screen, clearer table numbers

Familiar shape, easy to train on

Bulkier on the wrist through a shift

Higher cost per unit

Concept stage

Clip / pendant

Clips to an apron, collar or belt.

Nothing to strap on before a shift

Easiest to hand off between shifts

Smaller number display than the wristband

Vibration may be less noticeable than on the wrist

Day to day

What changes on the floor

No more shouting across the room

Calls reach the right person directly — no cupping hands or walking the floor to ask who saw table 9 flip.

No more scanning for raised hands

Stop sweeping the room "just in case." If your wrist hasn't buzzed, nobody in your section is waiting.

Coverage evens out automatically

Calls route to the assigned section, not to whoever notices first — so one server doesn't end up covering for three.

Less noise at peak

Fewer shouted handoffs during the rush — just a quiet buzz on the wrist that's meant for you.
